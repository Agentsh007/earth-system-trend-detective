Scientific Provenance

Methodology & Scientific Integrity

Complete scientific transparency and provenance documentation for all datasets and computational algorithms. Implements exact rank-based non-parametric statistics without reliance on language model calculations or stochastic heuristics. Every displayed metric traces back directly to authoritative NASA satellite missions and peer-reviewed methodologies.

1. Non-Parametric Trend Statistics

  • Mann-Kendall Test: Evaluates monotonic directional change over time. Tie corrections in observation ranks are strictly applied to variance calculations.
  • Theil-Sen Slope: Median of all pairwise slopes, resistant to outliers (breakdown point ≈ 29.3%). Converted to annual rate of change (e.g. °C/year).
  • 95% Confidence Interval: Hollander-Wolfe / Gilbert rank-based order statistics corresponding to α = 0.05.
  • Benjamini-Hochberg (FDR): Multi-region false discovery rate correction controlling false positive rates across all 64 districts.

2. NASA Datasets & Provenance

  • NASA POWER: Daily meteorology (MERRA-2 assimilation & GEOS/CERES radiation) for all 64 district centroids.
  • NASA MODIS (MOD13Q1): Gridded Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) tracking greenness and cropping cycles.
  • NASA GPM IMERG: Gridded satellite precipitation data capturing monsoon variability.
  • NASA JPL GRACE/GRACE-FO: Mascon RL06 terrestrial water storage anomalies tracking groundwater depletion.

3. Responsible Scientific Interpretation

Important Caveat: A p-value below 0.05 is not proof of causal attribution, nor does statistical significance automatically imply practical importance. Local micro-climates, sensor calibration changes, and multi-year climate cycles (such as ENSO and Indian Ocean Dipole) should always be evaluated alongside trend outputs.
